Task 1: Student Course Total
1. Input
- Assignment mark
- Midterm mark
- Final exam mark

2. Process
1. Declare variables for assignment, midterm, final marks, course total, and result.
2. Read the assignment, midterm, and final marks.
3. Calculate the course total using the given weights (20%, 30%, and 50%).
4. Use the conditional operator to determine whether the student passes or fails (pass mark = 50).
5. Display the course total to one decimal place and the result (Pass or Fail).
   
3. Output
- Course total.
- Pass or Fail status.

Task 2: Electricity Bill
1. Input
- Electricity units consumed (kWh).
  
2. Process
1. Declare variables for electricity units, energy charge, service charge, and total bill.
2. Initialize the service charge to 10 birr.
3. Read the electricity units consumed.
4. Calculate the energy charge using the conditional operator:
   - If units are less than or equal to 50, multiply units by 1.50.
   - Otherwise, calculate the cost of the first 50 units at 1.50 birr per unit and the remaining units at 2.00 birr per unit.
5. Calculate the total bill by adding the energy charge and service charge.
6. Display the energy charge, service charge, and total bill to two decimal places.
   
3. Output
- Energy charge.
- Service charge.
- Total electricity bill.

Task 3: Trip Speed and Fuel Cost
1. Input
- Trip distance (km).
- Travel time in hours.
- Travel time in minutes.
- Fuel consumption (liters per 100 km).
- Price per liter (birr).
  
2. Process
1. Declare variables for distance, travel hours, travel minutes, fuel consumption, price per liter, total travel time, average speed, fuel used, and fuel cost.
2. Read the trip distance, travel hours, travel minutes, fuel consumption, and price per liter.
3. Convert the travel time into total hours by adding the hours to the minutes divided by 60.
4. Calculate the average speed by dividing the distance by the total travel time.
5. Calculate the fuel used by multiplying the distance by fuel consumption and dividing by 100.
6. Calculate the fuel cost by multiplying the fuel used by the price per liter.
7. Display the average speed, fuel used, and fuel cost to two decimal places.
   
3. Output
- Average speed (km/h).
- Fuel used (liters).
- Fuel cost (birr).
